Harvest Foods Annual Report 2026

A year of steady growth. Harvest Foods packed and delivered grain, pulses and spices to 1,200 stores across western India. Revenue grew 12 percent, and we opened two new distribution centres in Nashik and Surat.

This report covers our people, our operations, our sustainability work and the year ahead.

Our people

We ended the year with 640 employees, up from 560. Training hours per person doubled, and our apprenticeship scheme placed 48 young people in full-time roles. Staff turnover fell to its lowest level in five years.

Sustainability

Solar panels on the warehouse roof cut power bills by 30 percent. The savings pay for new electric delivery vans, the first of which began routes in March.

We also moved to recycled packaging for all retail packs.

Outlook

Next year we plan to open a third distribution centre, widen our range of organic pulses and invest in cold storage for fresh produce.
